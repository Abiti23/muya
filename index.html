What is mUYA

mUYA helps you find skilled workers near you in Ethiopia and book them from your phone. Whether you need a plumber, an electrician, a cleaner, a mover, or someone to repair an appliance, you can find them in one place instead of asking around.

How it works

1Choose the service you need.
2Pick a worker from the list and see their rating and number of jobs.
3Choose a day and time, and add your address.
4The worker comes to you. After the job, you pay.

How you pay

You pay the worker directly, with telebirr or cash, after the job is done. mUYA never holds your money.

Your bookings and addresses

Your upcoming and past bookings are in the Bookings tab. Your payments are in the Wallet tab. Your saved addresses stay on your own phone.

Trust and safety

We are building a verified worker list. Workers marked SAMPLE are for testing only. If something goes wrong, tell us in Feedback.

Help us improve

mUYA is new, and your feedback shapes it. Use Feedback in your Profile to tell us what works and what doesn't.

Terms of Use

What mUYA is

mUYA connects customers with independent workers — plumbers, electricians, cleaners, and others. Workers on mUYA are not employees of mUYA. Each worker runs their own work and sets their own price.

Money

You pay the worker directly — with telebirr or cash. mUYA never holds your money and never takes payment from you.

Stay safe

mUYA is still growing: we are building a verified worker list. Meet workers in safe places. If something goes wrong, use Report a problem.

‹

Privacy Policy

What we store

Your phone number, name, city, and photo (if you add one). Also your saved addresses, bookings, messages, and job requests. For workers: work photos and ID photo. Your ID photo is private — customers never see it.

What we never do

We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Delete your data

In this demo version your data stays only on your own phone. To delete everything, go to Profile and tap Delete my data. When mUYA gets a server, you can ask us to delete your data at any time.

mUYA Guarantee

If a worker does not show up

Report the problem within 48 hours. We will call you and the worker to hear both sides.

If the work is poor

Tell us within 48 hours what went wrong. We will call both sides and ask the worker to fix it or agree on a fair solution with you.

If a worker behaves badly

Report it right away. Bad behavior is taken seriously: repeated problems lead to a permanent ban from mUYA.

About money

mUYA does not hold your money — you pay the worker directly. Because of this, mUYA cannot refund payments. We help you and the worker reach a fair agreement.
