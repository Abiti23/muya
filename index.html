What is mUYA

mUYA helps you find skilled workers near you in Ethiopia and book them from your phone. Whether you need a plumber, an electrician, a cleaner, a mover, or someone to repair an appliance, you can find them in one place instead of asking around.

How it works

1Choose the service you need.
2Pick a worker from the list and see their rating and number of jobs.
3Choose a day and time, and add your address.
4The worker comes to you. After the job, you pay.

How you pay

You pay the worker directly, with telebirr or cash, after the job is done. mUYA never holds your money.

Your bookings and addresses

Your upcoming and past bookings are in the Bookings tab. Your payments are in the Wallet tab. Your saved addresses stay on your own phone.

Trust and safety

We are building a verified worker list. Workers marked SAMPLE are for testing only. If something goes wrong, tell us in Feedback.

Help us improve

mUYA is new, and your feedback shapes it. Use Feedback in your Profile to tell us what works and what doesn't.

Privacy Policy

PRIVACY POLICY FOR THE USE OF THE MUYA APPLICATION

1. Our commitment

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy ("Policy"). This Policy describes what information mUYA stores, how it is used, and the choices available to you regarding your information.

2. Where your data lives

mUYA is currently a demo version. All your data is stored only on your own phone, inside your browser. mUYA has no server — your information is never sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nowhere to send it.

3. Collection of personal information

When you use mUYA, the application stores the following on your phone: i. Basic information (your name, phone number, city, and profile photo if you add one). ii. Your activity (saved addresses, bookings, messages, job requests, and marketplace listings). iii. For workers: work photos and ID photo. Your ID photo is private — customers never see it.

4. What we never collect

i. mUYA never collects payment details. mUYA never holds your money and has no payment license — you pay workers and sellers directly. ii. mUYA never collects your contacts, your location history, or any information you did not enter yourself.

5. Use of collected information

The information on your phone is used only to make the application work for you: i. Create and manage your account. ii. Show your bookings, messages, and listings. iii. Let workers manage their availability and job requests.

6. Disclosure of information

We do not share your personal data with anyone. There are no third parties, no partners, no advertisers — because mUYA has no server and sends your data nowhere. The only exception is when you choose to share your own information yourself, for example showing your phone number to a worker you booked.

7. Managing and deleting your data

You can edit your information at any time in your Profile. To delete everything, go to Profile and tap "Delete my data" — this permanently erases all mUYA data from your phone. When mUYA gets a server in the future, you will be able to ask us to delete your data at any time.

8. Information security

Because your data stays on your phone, its security depends on your phone's security — your screen lock, and who you let use your phone. Please take appropriate measures to protect your phone.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may change this Policy from time to time. When we do, the new version will appear in the application with a new "last updated" date. Your continued use of mUYA after a change means you accept it.

10. Acceptance of this Policy

By using the mUYA application, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy and agree to all its terms.

Contacting us

If you have any questions about this Policy, contact us in the application through Help and support. This document was last updated on October 17, 2026.
